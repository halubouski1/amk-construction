Most kitchen remodels in West Chester take 4 to 8 weeks from demolition to the final walkthrough. Planning, ordering and permits happen before that, so the full project usually starts a few weeks earlier.

What happens week by week

First the old kitchen comes out. Then plumbing and electrical are moved for the new layout, walls are patched and the room is ready for cabinets. Counters are templated once cabinets are set, then tile, lighting and trim finish the job.

Order of work

  1. Demolition of the old kitchen
  2. Rough‑in plumbing, gas and electrical
  3. Inspection, drywall and paint
  4. Cabinets set and leveled
  5. Countertops templated and installed
  6. Backsplash, lighting, trim and walkthrough

What can slow it down

Cabinet lead times are the biggest factor. Moving a sink or range adds plumbing and gas work. Permits in Chester County add time too, which is why we file them as soon as the estimate is approved.

Common delays

  • Cabinet lead times from the manufacturer
  • Layout changes that move a sink, range or gas line
  • Permit review in Chester County
  • Custom countertop fabrication

How to keep it on schedule

Pick cabinets, counters and tile before demolition starts. Keep one decision maker. And plan a temporary kitchen in another room: a microwave, coffee maker and fridge go a long way.

Before demolition

  • Choose cabinets, counters and tile
  • Agree on one decision maker
  • Set up a temporary kitchen

During the remodel

  • Keep a weekly check‑in with Alex
  • Approve changes in writing
