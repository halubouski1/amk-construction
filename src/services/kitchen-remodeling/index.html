From estimate
to first dinner

Most kitchens in West Chester take
4 to 8 weeks. Here is where
the time goes.

  1. 01

    Design and estimate

    We measure, talk through layout
    and finishes, and send a written estimate.

  2. 02

    Permits and ordering

    We pull permits and order cabinets
    and counters. Lead time depends on the cabinet line.

  3. 03

    Demolition and rough‑in

    Old kitchen out, plumbing and electrical moved where the new layout needs them.

  4. 04

    Cabinets, counters, tile

    Cabinets set, counters templated
    and installed, backsplash tiled.

  5. 05

    Finish and walkthrough

    Lighting, trim and touch‑ups, then a final walkthrough together.
