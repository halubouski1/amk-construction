The brief

The owners wanted a bright master bath with a soaking tub, a large walk‑in shower and an easy‑to‑clean floor, without losing the classic look of the house.

What we did

We took the room to the studs, moved the plumbing for a freestanding tub, built a frameless glass shower and finished walls and floor in large marble‑look porcelain.
