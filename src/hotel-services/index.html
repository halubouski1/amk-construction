How a hotel
project runs

  1. 01

    Walkthrough

    We walk the rooms and common areas with your manager and note what needs work.

  2. 02

    Scope and price per room

    A written scope and a price per room, so the budget is clear before we start.

  3. 03

    Floor‑by‑floor schedule

    Work is planned around occupancy, a few rooms or one floor at a time.

  4. 04

    Renovate and inspect

    Our crew renovates, then we inspect every room against the scope.

  5. 05

    Punch list and handover

    Final fixes, cleaning, and rooms handed back ready for guests.
